TARGETING MODES:

  • > T - Toggle Targeting HUD
  • > Arrows - Steer Pitch & Yaw
  • > Green Target: Ship Target
  • > Orange Target: Tethered for Orbit and Time Surfing

FLIGHT CONTROLS:

  • > W S - Warp to Target: Hold W until stop. Tap for Bursts.
  • > I O - Impulse Thrusters Forward / Reverse
  • > A D - Slide Left / Right
  • > F C - Slide Up / Down
  • > Q E - Roll Left / Roll Right
  • > SPACE - Inertial Brakes

CAMERA:

  • > Left Click + Drag - Orbit Camera
  • > Scroll Wheel - Zoom In / Out
  • > R - Cinematic Stay Camera Mode (Toggle On/Off) [NEW]

ADVANCED ORBITING [NEW]:

  • > V - Toggle Orbit
  • > Arrows (Moves Around Tethered Target)
  • > Hold Shift With Arrows - Rotates Ship With Tether Locked

TIME SURFING:

  • > \ - 1/4 Speed Time Forward [NEW]
  • > / - 1/2 Speed Time Forward
  • > ] [ - 1 to 9 Speed Time Forward / Reverse
  • > N - Return to Real Time

SYSTEMS:

  • > X - Swap Transport / Shuttle
  • > B - Docking Tractor Beam at Earth Space Station
  • > M - 3D Tactical Map Viewing Mouse Controls
  • > Z - Tactical Map Panning
